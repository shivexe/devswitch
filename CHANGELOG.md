# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-16

- Verification codes from SMS and notifications on the Mac: type them with Ctrl+Option+V or fill them with the Helium extension. Codes stay in memory and clear after 90 seconds.
- Incoming call panel with Decline and Silence.
- Phone notifications as Mac banners with reply, dismiss and mute.
- Send SMS from the Mac, Find my phone, and phone battery and network in the menu.
- Send files both ways, share links to the Mac, and a shared clipboard.
- Wireless control stays connected through sleep and restarts after the OS stops it.
- Prefer IPv6 link-local addresses on the local network, and restart the ADB server when the phone has no route.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
- Fix a crash in release builds caused by stripped Room database constructors.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-30

- Mac menu bar controls for Android developer options, USB debugging, and wireless debugging.
- Local encrypted QR pairing and background phone connection.
- Wireless screen sharing and input through ADB and scrcpy.
- Automatic wireless port updates on Android 14+.
- Redesigned Android dashboard with light and dark themes.
- Dependency detection, doctor command, and universal ad-hoc signed Mac builds.
