# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-30

- Mac menu bar controls for Android developer options, USB debugging, and wireless debugging.
- Local encrypted QR pairing and background phone connection.
- Wireless screen sharing and input through ADB and scrcpy.
- Automatic wireless port updates on Android 14+.
- Redesigned Android dashboard with light and dark themes.
- Dependency detection, doctor command, and universal ad-hoc signed Mac builds.
